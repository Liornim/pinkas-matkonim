Ingredients
* 6 Mission Carb Balance burrito tortillas
* 2 lbs 96/4 lean ground beef
* 1 small onion, finely diced
* 1 small bell pepper, finely diced
* 3 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
* 1% cups reduced-fat shredded mozzarella
*2 tsp salt
* 1 tsp garlic powder
* 1 tsp onion powder
* % tsp paprika
* Zero-calorie cooking spray
How To
1. Preheat oven to 420°F and lightly coat a baking dish
with zero-calorie cooking spray.
2. In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, onion, bell
pepper, parsley, 1 cup mozzarella, salt, garlic powder,
onion powder, and paprika.
3. Divide the mixture evenly between the 6 tortillas and
spread it into a thin layer, getting close to the edges.
4. Roll each tortilla tightly and slice into roughly 2%-inch
rolls.
5. Place the rolls cut-side up in the baking dish and
lightly spray the tops with cooking spray.
6. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until the beef is fully
cooked
7. Top with the remaining % cup mozzarella and bake
until melted. Broil for 2 minutes at the end for an extra
crispy top. @
